# Linhir and western Lebennin

This sheet continues east of Belfalas to Linhir and the Gilrain–Serni
junction. It fills part of the unpainted southeast without changing the
6030 × 7500 world grid. Pelargir and the remaining rivers of Lebennin are
reserved for a later sheet.

## Geographic reference

The relationships follow Christopher Tolkien’s [large-scale map of Rohan,
Gondor and Mordor](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/File:Christopher_Tolkien_-_Map_of_Rohan,_Gondor,_and_Mordor.png)
and *The Return of the King*, Book V, chapter 9, “The Last Debate”. Local
bends, fields, slopes and architecture remain pictorial interpretations;
this painting uses a nonuniform scale.

- [Gilrain](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Gilrain) separates Belfalas from
  Lebennin. It joins the Serni before the combined waters reach the sea.
- [Serni](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Serni) rises south of the eastern
  White Mountains and flows southwest into the Gilrain above Linhir. It is
  not an Anduin tributary. Sources differ in the name used for their joint
  lower course; the atlas describes it as the combined waters.
- [Linhir](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Linhir) stands on the west bank
  below the confluence. The road from Erech passes through it toward
  Pelargir. The narrative names fords; Tolkien’s unfinished index describes
  a haven with a ferrybridge. The painting suggests a modest crossing and
  landings without asserting a documented bridge design or town plan.
- [Lebennin](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Lebennin) lies east of Gilrain,
  south of the White Mountains, and west of Anduin. This sheet shows its
  western countryside only. The landscape marker denotes a region, not
  an invented settlement.
- Anduin continues from the established southern channel and exits the
  eastern edge. It does not end in an invented lake or river mouth here.
  Pelargir, Sirith, Celos and Erui are not placed in this partial sheet.

For the later eastern sheet, the large-scale map takes precedence over the
1980 small map’s acknowledged error: Celos joins Sirith from the west.

## Rendering

The new artwork sits beneath the old painting. Existing opaque terrain,
water samples and shoreline wave cells keep their coordinates. River motion
follows the inspected channels and coastal motion extends the shared wave
field. The existing global day clock, viewport-sized renderer, 30 fps cap
and hidden/paused sleep remain unchanged. No separate regional lighting or
animation loop is introduced. Asset download and decoded storage grow with
the additional painted coverage, while the world texture dimensions stay
the same.
