# Belfalas and the havens

This sheet continues the southern coast from Anfalas and Lamedon to
Edhellond, Cobas Haven, Dol Amroth and the Belfalas peninsula. The existing
world coordinates are retained; the southern extent grows from 5550 to
7500. Lebennin, Linhir and the mouths of Anduin remain beyond this sheet.

## Geographic reference

Christopher Tolkien’s [The West of Middle-earth at the End of the Third
Age](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/File:Christopher_Tolkien_-_The_West_of_Middle-earth.png)
provides the geographic relationships. This atlas remains a pictorial map
with nonuniform scale; individual coves, slopes and architecture are
interpretations rather than documented street plans.

- The Ringló joins the Morthond before their combined waters reach the sea.
  The two northern channels continue from their established coordinates.
- [Edhellond](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Edhellond) lies southwest of
  the confluence, near the river mouth, north of Dol Amroth. Its last Elven
  ship departed in Third Age 1981. The late Third Age painting shows a quiet
  abandoned haven, with no active Elven settlement or fleet. Reference:
  *Unfinished Tales*, “The History of Galadriel and Celeborn”, “Amroth and
  Nimrodel”; *The Adventures of Tom Bombadil*, preface.
- [Cobas Haven](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Cobas_Haven) receives the
  combined river mouth. Dol Amroth’s headland forms its southern boundary.
- [Dol Amroth](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Dol_Amroth) occupies an
  attached high headland on the western side of Belfalas. Its castle,
  port-city and Sea-ward Tower are distinct from the older Elven haven.
  Reference: *The Return of the King*, “Minas Tirith”; *The Adventures of
  Tom Bombadil*, preface.
- [Belfalas](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Belfalas) lies south of Lamedon
  and east of the lower Morthond/Ringló. Its mountainous promontory extends
  into the sea. The eastern boundary at Gilrain is outside this sheet.
- The established road toward Linhir continues out of the eastern edge.
  No game-only towns, islands, named mountain ranges or invented major
  road branches are supplied.

## Rendering

The sheet is composited beneath the prior painting. Earlier opaque terrain
and water samples retain their coordinates. New coverage extends the shared
river surface and shoreline wave field; no regional lighting layer or
separate animation clock is added. The existing viewport surface limits,
30 fps cap and hidden/paused sleep remain in force. The added painting and
coverage increase initial download and decoded asset storage.
