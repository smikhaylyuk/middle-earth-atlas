# Eriador style harmonization

The original western, eastern and southern Eriador sheets have darker ochre
midtones and denser etched grain than the later Anduin, Rohan and Rauros
paintings. The comparison used the baked daylight image at the same scale;
this difference exists independently of the animated clock. Golden Lórien,
the Brown Lands, dark forests and cultivated Shire fields remain deliberate
landscape differences.

One built-in ImageGen edit produced a lighter olive/sage palette study from
the existing 2500 × 1940 Eriador crop, with Anduin/Rohan and Rauros as style
references. The output was 1424 × 1105 and reinterpreted small hills and
water edges. It is retained as
`public/images/repairs/eriador-style-reference.webp`, but none of its spatial
features are composited into the map. Its exact prompt is recorded below.

`scripts/harmonize-atlas.mjs` takes only the study's land colour distribution.
It applies a restrained, monotone tone adjustment and reduces fine etched
grain in the original land pixels. All relief and vegetation remain registered
to the existing painting. Water, bank buffers, recorded road corridors and
landmark anchors are protected, with broad feathered transitions. The change
fades out before the Anduin and Rohan sheet edges. Original alpha is retained.

The displayed result is `public/images/atlas-harmonized.webp` (4350 × 3300).
It is the final stage of `npm run build:atlas`; `atlas-expanded.webp` is kept
as the unchanged geographic base. Neither water masks nor motion guides are
regenerated by the style pass. The runtime still uses one viewport canvas,
the same shared day/night wash, and the existing river and shore animations.

Regression checks exercise the displayed asset: exact world dimensions and
alpha, protected water/roads/landmarks, unchanged newer regions (allowing only
WebP recompression), a measured midtone/colour improvement, and reduced fine
grain without erasing relief. Existing flow tests sample this final painting.
The pass makes no new claim about the canonicity of incidental brushwork.

## Exact built-in ImageGen prompt

Use case: style-transfer.
Asset type: seamless painted Tolkien atlas region; geography is an exact registration constraint.

EDIT ONLY IMAGE 1, eriador-target.png. Image 1 is the complete 2500 × 1940 target composition and is the sole authority for every geographic feature. Images 2 (anduin-rohan-style.png) and 3 (rauros-style.png) are supporting references ONLY for palette, local contrast, and painterly brush treatment. Do not transfer any geography, object, waterfall, river, forest footprint, or composition from the references.

Primary request: harmonize the original western Eriador painting with the newer reference regions. Keep the exact framing, scale, orientation, boundaries, positions and proportions of image 1; request the same 2500 × 1940 canvas at the highest available fidelity. Repaint the existing surfaces in a cleaner, softly painted atlas style without redesigning the map.

Change only palette, local contrast, and surface brush treatment: lighten the muddy ochre and gold-brown midtones into restrained olive, sage, and muted golden grass, with neutral grey rock relief. Break up the old dense scratchy etched hill marks and tiny repeated dark flecks by replacing that high-frequency surface noise with coherent organic soft terrain shading, readable vegetation masses, and natural tree groupings at their existing locations. Preserve painted detail and legibility: no uniform green recoloring, blur, fog or wash. Match the references' calmer open ground and clear grey rocks, maintaining a fine hand-painted quality.

EXACT INVARIANTS: do not shift, widen, narrow, straighten, reroute or redraw the coastline, bays, estuaries, any river or brook, lake outlines, tributary network, islands, wetlands, road routes, crossings, mountain ridge positions, forest footprints, town footprints, ruins or buildings. Preserve every existing water connection and every existing visible detail of these sensitive geographic features. Preserve the distinctive central Old Forest, the cultivated patchwork of the Shire to its west, and the more open southern lands. Keep the continuous eastern mountains in their original positions and preserve all small hills in place.

Water remains cool blue/teal, including rivers, lakes, sea and marshes. Keep gentle natural differences between muted olive grasslands, golden cultivated land, dark olive woodland and grey rock. Use soft uniform neutral daylight throughout, with no dramatic sunlight or new directional shadows.

No added labels, text, words, UI, compass, symbols, roads, brooks, buildings, mountains, trees outside existing vegetation locations, new terrain features or invented details. No new season. Output one full edited version of image 1 with its complete original composition, no border, no crop, no zoom, no rotation.
