# The western coast of Gondor

This sheet fills the open southwest of the existing 6030 × 5550 atlas,
west of Blackroot Vale and Lamedon. It adds the wooded Drúwaith Iaur,
the Lefnui, Pinnath Gelin and the western shore of Anfalas.

## Geographic reference

The reference is Christopher Tolkien’s [The West of Middle-earth at the End
of the Third Age](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/File:Christopher_Tolkien_-_The_West_of_Middle-earth.png).
The atlas keeps its existing pictorial scale and coordinates; local bends,
tree cover and individual hill shapes are interpreted, not surveyed.

- [Drúwaith Iaur](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Dr%C3%BAwaith_Iaur) occupies
  the northwestern side of the mountains of the Andrast promontory. It is
  distinct from Drúadan Forest near Minas Tirith and was not settled by
  Gondor. Reference: *Unfinished Tales*, “The Drúedain”, further notes.
- The mountainous Andrast promontory remains connected to the mainland.
  Its western and southern shores meet the sea; it is not an island.
- The [Lefnui](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Lefnui) rises in the western
  White Mountains and flows southwest to a long, narrow firth. It remains
  west of Pinnath Gelin, separate from both the Isen and Morthond.
- [Pinnath Gelin](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Pinnath_Gelin) is a range
  of green hills north of Anfalas, between Lefnui and Morthond.
- [Anfalas](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Anfalas), or Langstrand, is the
  coastal fief south of those hills. Neither a capital nor an invented
  highway is supplied where the published map identifies none.

The selectable regional markers indicate landscape areas, not cities.
Place notes distinguish book geography from pictorial detail and link to
references. No game-only settlements or invented crossings are added.

## Rendering

The new sheet is composited beneath the established painting, preserving
opaque terrain and filling its feathered boundaries. It uses the existing
shared lighting, cached viewport painting and water-surface renderer.
The canvas dimensions and animation frame limit remain unchanged.
The additional artwork increases download size, but adds no full-map
render surface or independent day/night layer.
