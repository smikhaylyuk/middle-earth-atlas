# Erech, Lamedon and the Ringló crossing

The new sheet fills the southern slopes of the White Mountains within the
existing 6030 × 5550 atlas. It connects beneath Rohan and western Anórien,
with Erech, Tarlang’s Neck, Calembel and Ethring as selectable destinations.
The later route to Linhir and Pelargir continues beyond the southern edge.

## Reference and geographic constraints

Christopher Tolkien’s [Map of Rohan, Gondor, and Mordor](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/File:Christopher_Tolkien_-_Map_of_Rohan,_Gondor,_and_Mordor.png)
is the geographic reference. Narrative details come from *The Return of the
King*, Book V, “The Passing of the Grey Company” and “The Last Debate”.

- [Erech](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Erech) lies south of the mountains,
  at the mouth of Blackroot Vale. Its hill bears a smooth black globe. The
  stone is enlarged for visibility; it is not a tower or standing monolith.
- [Tarlang’s Neck](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Tarlang%27s_Neck) crosses a
  southern mountain spur between Blackroot Vale and Lamedon. No invented
  surface road represents the underground Paths of the Dead.
- [Calembel](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Calembel) is a modest hill-town
  east of the Ciril ford. The river runs south to join the Ringló.
- [Ethring](https://tolkiengateway.net/wiki/Ethring) is the Ringló crossing
  east and somewhat south of Calembel. The published map indicates a bridge,
  without identifying a town. The painting therefore does not add a city.
- The Morthond lies west of the Ciril and Ringló. Both the Morthond and lower
  Ringló continue beyond this sheet; their downstream meeting is outside
  the current extent. Rivers do not end arbitrarily inside the new valleys.

The atlas uses pictorial, nonuniform scale rather than a surveyed projection.
The existing Dunharrow, White Mountain ridge and Minas Tirith fix the new
sheet’s registration. Their positions remain unchanged. Individual mountains,
road bends, field boundaries and building designs are artistic interpretation.
The scene is not a depiction of the Grey Company’s dated march; no army of
the Dead or later political settlement has been added.

## Rendering and preservation

The new painting is composited beneath the old atlas, filling unpainted
territory and the earlier edge fades. Previously opaque terrain is protected.
Two small insets correct the Ciril ford and separate the Morthond spring
from Rohan’s northern drainage with a dry mountain divide. The shared
water grid retains every prior positive sample. New flow guides
follow inspected channels, with land and the bridge excluded by the mask.

The full-resolution painting cache, prepared water shader, visibility culling,
30 fps animation limit and sleeping hidden/paused renderer remain in use.
The atlas dimensions and presentation resolution are unchanged. Additional
terrain increases the compressed download and region-mask storage; it does
not introduce another full-map canvas or regional lighting layer.
